About Jim Karwisch

I’m Jim Karwisch, and I work with smart, capable people who feel stuck in ways that don’t make sense on paper.

They have experience. They have ideas. They often have success already. What they are missing is traction. Something inside keeps pulling against the direction they want to move.

My work focuses on that internal friction.

Over the last decade, I’ve helped founders, leaders, creatives, and operators untangle the stories shaping how they think, decide, communicate, and act. Stories about responsibility. About confidence. About what they are allowed to want. About how much space they can take up. When those stories stay unexamined, progress stalls. When they are brought into the open, momentum returns.

Untangled Narrative is built around a simple belief: clarity creates agency. When you can see the narrative you are operating from, you gain options. You can test new approaches instead of repeating old patterns. You can build skills instead of forcing outcomes. You can trust your judgment instead of outsourcing it.

My background spans coaching, facilitation, narrative strategy, and skill-building across leadership, communication, decision-making, and emotional regulation. I do not hand clients answers. I help them develop the ability to think clearly under pressure, make aligned choices, and move forward without needing constant reassurance.

The goal is durable change. Confidence that holds up in real conditions. Skills that compound. A sense of authorship over your own direction.

That is the work.
